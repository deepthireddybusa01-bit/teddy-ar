# Teddy AR

A cute pink teddy bear that waves hi, pulls a heart keychain from its pocket, blows a kiss
(hearts, a gift box and an "I LOVE YOU" letter), with butterflies and a plant.

Open the page on an Android phone in Chrome and tap **View in AR** to place the teddy in your room.
On a computer, drag to look around and tap the teddy to replay the show.

- Teddy model: original, made procedurally for this project
- Rig and animations: Adobe Mixamo
- Rendering: three.js
